# Favorable-Regime Hypothesis — UNTESTED

This document records a future hypothesis only. It is **UNTESTED** and is not a result of the preserved CFHM execution.

## Hypothesis

The transmission channel may become identifiable and trainable in a favorable regime if the transmission coefficient scale `b*` is increased several-fold, the calibrated intercept `gamma` is nearer zero, and `lambda1` is floored for the transmission parameters `b` rather than allowed to suppress them at the observed scale.

## Why this is not a finding

The shipped CFHM regime produced a documented collapse: the channel amplitudes stayed near initialization while the oracle signal remained rankable. The hypothesis above changes the regime and therefore would require a new binding specification, new seeds or a pre-registered extension, new acceptance criteria, and a new evidence tree. No rerun, tuning, or repair is authorized by this package.

## Boundary

Do not present this memo as evidence that CFHM works in the favorable regime. The current artifact is a negative result and is not a working hazard predictor. Any future test must preserve this artifact unchanged and obtain author approval before execution.
